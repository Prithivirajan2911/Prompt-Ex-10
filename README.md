# Ex.No.10: Content Creation (Reports, Articles, Case Studies, etc.) Using Prompt Patterns

## Aim:

To demonstrate how different prompting techniques such as query decomposition, decision making, answer engineering, fact checking, tail generation, menu actions, and semantic filtering can be used to create high-quality structured and creative content using ChatGPT.

---

# Prompt Patterns Used:

* **Query Decomposition:** Breaking a complex task into smaller steps.
* **Decision Making:** Asking the model to select the most suitable option.
* **Answer Engineering:** Controlling the structure and format of the response.
* **Fact Check List:** Verifying important factual information.
* **Tail Generation:** Extending existing content with additional relevant details.
* **Menu Actions:** Providing multiple choices and selecting an appropriate direction.
* **Semantic Filter:** Controlling tone, style, relevance, and content quality.

---

# Test Case 1: Case Study on Business Success

## Topic:

### Success of an Eco-Friendly Startup

## Prompt Refinement Progression

### Iteration 1 (Basic Prompt):

`Write a case study about a successful eco-friendly startup.`

### Iteration 2 (Query Decomposition Prompt):

`Create a case study about an eco-friendly startup by breaking the topic into smaller sections: business idea, target customers, products or services, marketing strategy, challenges, growth factors, and reasons for success.`

### Iteration 3 (Decision Making Prompt):

`Consider three possible growth strategies for the eco-friendly startup: (A) focus on online sales, (B) expand through physical retail stores, and (C) partner with established businesses. Select the most suitable strategy for a growing startup and explain the reasoning.`

### Iteration 4 (Answer Engineering Prompt):

`Create a structured business case study with the following sections: Background, Business Idea, Target Market, Strategy, Challenges, Growth Factors, and Lessons Learned. Use a professional and analytical tone. Present the information using clear headings and concise paragraphs.`

### Iteration 5 (Fact Check List Prompt):

`Review the case study and check whether the claims are logically consistent and whether any unsupported statistics, specific financial figures, or unverifiable facts have been included. Remove or clearly qualify unsupported information.`

### Iteration 6 (Semantic Filter Prompt):

`Refine the case study for a college-level business report. Maintain a formal, objective and analytical tone. Focus on business strategy, customer value, sustainability, challenges and success factors. Remove repetition and unnecessary promotional language.`

## Generated Final Content

### Background

The case study focuses on an eco-friendly startup that develops sustainable household products using environmentally responsible materials. The startup aims to provide practical alternatives to commonly used disposable products.

### Business Idea

The company identifies increasing customer interest in sustainable lifestyles as an opportunity. It develops reusable and environmentally friendly products that combine functionality, affordability and sustainability.

### Target Market

The primary target customers are environmentally conscious consumers, students, working professionals and households interested in reducing their use of disposable products.

### Business Strategy

The startup initially focuses on online sales because this approach requires lower infrastructure costs and allows the company to reach customers across different locations.

The company uses social media content, educational campaigns and customer reviews to communicate the environmental value of its products.

### Major Challenges

* Establishing customer trust as a new brand
* Managing product and material costs
* Competing with established companies
* Educating customers about sustainable alternatives
* Maintaining consistent product quality

### Factors Behind Success

The startup's success can be associated with several factors:

* Clear identification of a customer problem
* Strong sustainability-focused value proposition
* Effective digital marketing
* Customer-oriented product development
* Continuous improvement based on feedback

### Lessons Learned

The case demonstrates that a successful startup requires more than a good product idea. Understanding customer needs, selecting an appropriate business strategy, communicating value effectively and adapting to challenges are important for sustainable business growth.

# Test Case 2: Product Launch Announcement

## Topic:

### Launch of an AI-Powered Smartwatch

## Prompt Refinement Progression

### Iteration 1 (Basic Prompt):

`Write a product launch announcement for an AI-powered smartwatch.`

### Iteration 2 (Query Decomposition Prompt):

`Create a product launch announcement by dividing the content into product introduction, key features, target users, benefits, technology, availability, and call to action.`

### Iteration 3 (Menu Actions Prompt):

`Choose the most suitable launch style from these options: (A) formal corporate announcement, (B) energetic promotional announcement, (C) technology-focused announcement. Select the best option for launching an AI-powered smartwatch to young professionals and explain the choice.`

### Iteration 4 (Tail Generation Prompt):

`Expand the product announcement by adding details about how the AI assistant helps users manage daily activities, track fitness goals, receive reminders, and interact with the smartwatch. Maintain a clear connection between each feature and its user benefit.`

### Iteration 5 (Answer Engineering + Fact Check Prompt):

`Create a professional product launch announcement with the sections: Headline, Product Introduction, Key Features, User Benefits, Availability, and Call to Action. Do not claim medical benefits or specific performance figures unless they are provided. Clearly distinguish product capabilities from promotional language.`

### Iteration 6 (Final Semantic Filter Prompt):

`Refine the announcement for young professionals and technology users. Use an energetic but professional tone. Keep the content concise, informative and engaging. Highlight AI assistance, productivity, fitness tracking and personalization. Avoid exaggerated claims and unnecessary technical jargon.`

## Generated Final Content

### Introducing the SmartWatch AI

Technology is becoming increasingly personal, and the new SmartWatch AI is designed to bring intelligent assistance directly to your wrist.

### Intelligent Personal Assistant

The smartwatch includes an AI-powered assistant that can help users manage reminders, organize daily activities and provide quick information through a convenient wearable interface.

### Key Features

* AI-powered personal assistance
* Fitness and activity tracking
* Smart reminders and notifications
* Personalized daily insights
* Smartphone connectivity
* Modern lightweight design

### User Benefits

The SmartWatch AI is designed for users who want to manage daily tasks while staying connected and active.

The intelligent assistant can help users organize reminders and activities, while fitness features allow them to monitor everyday activity and exercise-related information.

### Designed for Modern Users

The product is aimed at students, professionals and technology enthusiasts who want a combination of productivity, connectivity and personal activity tracking in a wearable device.

### Availability

The SmartWatch AI will be introduced through selected online and retail channels. Availability and product specifications may vary depending on the final product configuration.

### Call to Action

Experience a smarter way to manage your everyday activities with SmartWatch AI.

**Stay connected. Stay active. Stay organized.**

# Comparison of Prompting Techniques

| **Prompt Technique**    | **Effect on Output**                                       |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Basic Prompt**        | Produces a general response with limited structure         |
| **Query Decomposition** | Breaks complex content into manageable sections            |
| **Decision Making**     | Helps select the most appropriate strategy or direction    |
| **Answer Engineering**  | Improves structure, organization and presentation          |
| **Fact Check List**     | Reduces unsupported or inconsistent claims                 |
| **Tail Generation**     | Adds depth and relevant details to existing content        |
| **Menu Actions**        | Provides multiple possible directions before selecting one |
| **Semantic Filter**     | Controls tone, relevance, style and content quality        |

---

# Deliverables & Key Findings

* **First Draft:** Generated using a simple basic prompt.
* **Refined Content:** Improved by adding structure, details and constraints.
* **Multiple Versions:** Different prompt patterns produced different styles and levels of detail.
* **Final Version:** Created by combining multiple prompting techniques.
* **Prompt Specificity Matters:** Detailed instructions produce more controlled and relevant outputs.
* **Iterative Refinement:** Repeatedly improving the prompt increases the quality and usefulness of generated content.
* **Different Patterns Have Different Roles:** Business case studies benefit from decomposition, decision making and fact checking, while product announcements benefit from answer engineering, tail generation, menu actions and semantic filtering.

---

# Result

The experiment successfully demonstrated the use of multiple prompt patterns for generating structured business content. Query decomposition improved organization, decision making helped select suitable directions, answer engineering controlled the output format, fact checking improved reliability, tail generation added depth, menu actions provided alternatives, and semantic filtering improved tone and relevance.

The two test cases demonstrated how the same AI model can produce different types of content when appropriate prompting techniques are applied.

The experiment demonstrates that **structured and iterative prompting provides greater control over AI-generated content and can be effectively applied to different content-creation requirements.**
